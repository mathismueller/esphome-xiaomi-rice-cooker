


Summary of Findings & Work Completed

1. Hardware Architecture & Silicon Identification

⚬ Device Model: Xiaomi Mi Induction Heating Rice Cooker (IHFB01CM / chunmi.cooker.normal2).
⚬ Wireless Module: Xiaomi MHCW03P (CMIIT ID: 2016DP1293), MAC 34:ce:00:9a:f0:27 (Espressif OUI).
⚬ SoC: Espressif ESP8285N08 (Xtensa LX106 @ 160 MHz) with internal embedded flash.
⚬ Flash Geometry: 2 MByte (16 Mbit) SPI Flash, JEDEC ID c8 4015 (GigaDevice GD25Q16 compatible).
⚬ Main Appliance Controller: Independent ARM Cortex-M MCU (IC1).
⚬ Architecture Distinction vs. MFB120A-1:
  ⚬ In resistive cookers like the MFB120A-1, the ESP32 acts as the master brain driving dumb shift registers/relays.
  ⚬ In the IHFB01CM, the appliance MCU is fully autonomous, managing the induction resonant inverter, thermal safeties, buttons, and TM1620 LED matrix. The ESP8285 acts purely as an IoT telemetry bridge.

3. Protocol Discovery

From serial captures on GPIO2, the communication link between the ESP8285 and the appliance MCU uses plain ASCII and JSON:

⚬ Downlink (ESP \rightarrow MCU): Plain ASCII lines terminated by \r:
  ⚬ down get_prop "all"\r
  ⚬ down MIIO_net_change local\r
⚬ Uplink (MCU \rightarrow ESP): Direct JSON array responses:
  {"result":["waiting","0001","null","22","60","-1","60","0607","050415","0003001a","0103","ffff11..."]}
  
  ⚬ Index 0: State (waiting, running, pause, error)
  ⚬ Index 1: Cooking profile (0001 = standard rice)
  ⚬ Index 3: Temperature (22°C ambient)
  ⚬ Index 4: Remaining duration (60 minutes)

4. Firmware Backup Status

⚬ Bench Power: Powered safely via 5.0 V DC bench power supply with AC mains unplugged (~80–250 mA draw).
⚬ Baud Rate Resolution: Initial reads at 460800 baud failed at 1.84 MB due to signal degradation over jumper wires. Dropping to 230400 baud completed the full 2,097,152-byte (2 MB) read without errors:
  esptool --port /dev/cu.usbserial-110 -b 230400 read-flash 0x0 0x200000 stock_backup_2MB.bin
